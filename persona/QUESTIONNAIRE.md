# Persona Questionnaire

Answer in rough notes. Bullet points, half sentences and "umm, I guess…" are all fine.
**Specific stories and real details matter far more than polished wording.** The bot
turns these into spoken answers, so what you give it is everything it knows about you.

> ⚠️ The repo will be public, so this content will be too. Only write what you're happy
> for anyone to read. Leave out anything private (salary, health, family conflicts, etc.).

Rough time: 60–90 minutes. If you're short on time, do the ★ sections first.
